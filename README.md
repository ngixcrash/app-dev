## Favorite movie: Spirited Away

*Spirited Away* follows Chihiro, a girl who takes a job at a bathhouse for spirits after her parents are transformed into pigs. She has to navigate the bathhouse's strange rules while finding a way to bring her family home.

### What stands out

- **Chihiro's courage:** She starts out frightened and grows more resourceful with each challenge.
- **The bathhouse:** The bustling staff and unusual guests make the spirit world feel lived-in.
- **The train scene:** Its quiet pacing gives the story room to breathe after the noise of the bathhouse.

> My takeaway: Courage can start with doing the next small thing even when you are scared.
